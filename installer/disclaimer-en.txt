BEFORE YOU INSTALL

GlucoBar shows the glucose reading of your sensor on your PC: FreeStyle Libre,
read through LibreLinkUp (Abbott's sharing service), or Dexcom, read through
Dexcom Share (beta).

• GlucoBar is NOT a medical device. Do not use it to decide insulin doses or to
  treat a hypo: always rely on your reader or the official app.
• It uses unofficial APIs. Abbott and Dexcom can change them at any time and
  GlucoBar may stop working without notice.
• Readings may arrive a few minutes late, or not at all if the phone is away
  from the sensor or offline. Old readings (more than 15 minutes) are shown in grey.
• GlucoBar is an independent project, not affiliated with or endorsed by Abbott
  or Dexcom. FreeStyle, Libre, LibreLink and LibreLinkUp are trademarks of Abbott.
  Dexcom is a trademark of Dexcom, Inc.

WHAT YOU NEED
• FreeStyle Libre: a LibreLinkUp account that "follows" your sensor. In the
  LibreLink app invite an email address (preferably a different one from yours),
  then accept the invitation and the terms of use in the LibreLinkUp app.
• Dexcom: your Dexcom account, with Share turned on in the Dexcom app and at
  least one follower.

Your password is stored encrypted in Windows Credential Manager and is sent only
to the servers of your sensor's maker (Abbott LibreView or Dexcom Share).
