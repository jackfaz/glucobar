BEFORE YOU INSTALL

GlucoBar shows the glucose reading of your FreeStyle Libre sensor on your PC,
read through LibreLinkUp, Abbott's sharing service.

• GlucoBar is NOT a medical device. Do not use it to decide insulin doses or to
  treat a hypo: always rely on your reader or the official app.
• It uses an unofficial API. Abbott can change it at any time and GlucoBar may
  stop working without notice.
• Readings may arrive a few minutes late, or not at all if the phone is away
  from the sensor or offline. Old readings (more than 15 minutes) are shown in grey.
• GlucoBar is an independent project, not affiliated with or endorsed by Abbott.
  FreeStyle, Libre, LibreLink and LibreLinkUp are trademarks of Abbott.

WHAT YOU NEED
A LibreLinkUp account that "follows" your sensor: in the LibreLink app invite an
email address (preferably a different one from yours), then accept the invitation
and the terms of use in the LibreLinkUp app.

Your password is stored encrypted in Windows Credential Manager and is sent only
to Abbott's LibreView servers.
